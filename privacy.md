# Peppa Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Peppa Team ("Peppa," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the Peppa mobile app and the peppa.games website (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It is part of our Terms of Service.

## 1. Who We Are

The Service is operated by the Peppa Team. You can reach us at <info.peppa.games@gmail.com>. We will provide our operator name and address without delay if you ask us at that address.

The Service is for people aged 13 and over. See Section 10.

## 2. Information We Collect

**When you create an account.** You sign in with Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a user ID, your email address (a private relay address if you chose to hide your email), and, the first time only, your name, which we use to fill in your display name. We never receive or store your Apple Account password.

**Your profile and activity.** We store the display name and handle shown on your profile, your avatar (the address of a game cover image and how you positioned it), when your account was created, and how many people you follow and are followed by. We also store what you do in the app: your game library and its statuses, completion records including dates, ratings, and short comments, reviews, comments, likes, lists, votes, follows, blocks, and game requests you send or receive.

**Reports.** When you report a review, comment, or user, we store who reported it, what was reported, the reason, your message, and a copy of the reported text as it appeared at that moment, so we can review it even if the content is later edited or deleted.

**Advertising.** The app may show ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob may receive your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how you use the app. See Section 6.

**On your device only.** Items you save to Later (their links, titles, images, and any text you shared), games you add manually, and a record of what you have reported are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

**Connections your device makes.** When you search for or open a game, your device contacts IGDB (operated by Twitch Interactive), which receives your search words and your IP address. When you save a link, your device contacts that website directly to read its title and image.

**Our website.** peppa.games sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub, which may record requests to its servers, including IP addresses, for security and operations.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we have turned Firebase Analytics off. We do not ask for your location, photos, contacts, microphone, or camera. We do not sell your information.

## 4. How We Use Information

- To provide the Service: to show your profile, library, reviews, and lists, and to deliver follows and game requests.
- To keep Peppa safe: to review reports, apply blocks, hide or remove content, and suspend accounts that break our rules.
- To answer you when you contact us.
- To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet our legal obligations.
- To show ads, and to let you remove them if you buy the ad-free option.

## 5. What Other People Can See

The following are visible to other Peppa users: your profile (display name, handle, and avatar); your game library, except games you mark as archived; your completion records on beaten games, including completion dates, ratings, and short comments; reviews you post; comments; lists and votes; and your follows. Game requests are visible only to the person you send them to. Blocks and reports are never shown to the other person.

## 6. Advertising

We may show ads in the app, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized, which means AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and information about your activity to choose which ads you see. Google's policy explains how Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

**Your choices.**

- The app asks for your permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
- You can change your answer at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
- You can also turn on Limit Ad Tracking style controls under Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Apple Advertising.
- If we offer an ad-free purchase, buying it removes ads from the app.

## 7. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card number or payment details. We use RevenueCat to record what you have bought and whether it is still active; RevenueCat receives a purchase identifier and your app user ID for that purpose.

## 8. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

- **Apple** — sign-in and payments.
- **Google (Firebase)** — to store your account and the content you post.
- **Google (AdMob)** — advertising, as described in Section 6.
- **IGDB / Twitch Interactive** — game information; they receive your searches and IP address.
- **RevenueCat** — purchase records.
- **Authorities** — where the law requires it, or to address illegal content or a serious risk to someone's safety.

## 9. Where Your Information Is Stored

Your account and the content you post are stored in Google's Firebase services in the **United States** (the Firestore `nam5` multi-region, which covers data centers in Iowa and South Carolina). Sending information there is a transfer of personal data to a third party in a foreign country under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information. The United States has an established data protection framework. Google describes the safeguards it applies at <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

Our other providers may also process your information outside your country.

## 10. How Long We Keep Information

- **Your account and content:** until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time from the app, which deletes your profile, library, completion records, ratings, reviews, comments, likes, lists, votes, follows, blocks, and game requests.
- **Reports:** one year from the day we receive them, after which they are deleted automatically. We keep them this long so we can recognize repeated behavior. Reports are not deleted when the person who made them deletes their account.
- **Backups:** residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion.
- We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

## 11. Children

The Service is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use Peppa only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

## 12. Security

We protect your information with access rules that let each person write only their own data, and all communication with our servers is encrypted in transit. Sign in with Apple means we never handle your password. No service can be completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

## 13. Your Rights and Choices

- **See and change your information:** your profile and content are in the app, where you can edit or delete them.
- **Delete everything:** use Delete account in the app.
- **Ask us:** you can ask us to disclose, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop using it, by emailing <info.peppa.games@gmail.com>. We will respond without undue delay, and we may need to confirm your identity first.
- **Ads:** see the choices in Section 6.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with a new "Last updated" date, and we will tell you in the app or by other reasonable means before an important change takes effect.
